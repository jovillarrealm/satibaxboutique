# Satibax Boutique

E-commerce catalog and content platform for a natural cosmetics boutique, migrating from Hostinger to Cloudflare Pages.

## Language

**Product**:
A natural cosmetic, personal care, or wellness item displayed in the catalog with name, description, brand, price, images, and category.
_Avoid_: SKU, article, item

**Kit**:
A curated bundle or gift package containing multiple products (featured in the "Regalos" section).
_Avoid_: Combo, pack

**Item Selection**:
The curated list of products and quantities assembled by the customer to request via WhatsApp, rather than an automated transaction checkout.
_Avoid_: Cart, shopping cart, basket, draft checkout

**WhatsApp Order**:
The inquiry and ordering mechanism where the customer's Item Selection is formatted into an itemized WhatsApp message sent to the boutique's phone number to coordinate stock, payment method, and shipping in direct conversation.
_Avoid_: Automated checkout, gateway transaction, order submission, online payment

**Store Admin**:
The boutique operator responsible for updating products, pricing, stock status, images, and blog articles via a web-based dashboard.
_Avoid_: Webmaster, developer, system administrator

**Subscriber**:
A visitor who submits their email address to receive newsletters and boutique promotions.
_Avoid_: Lead, user, customer account
